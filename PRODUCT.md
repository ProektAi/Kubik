# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Personal use by the site owner and guests who need a quick, believable dice roll, card draw, or Jyotish dasha randomizer in the browser. The user is focused on one task: choose the mode, choose the amount or period depth, and roll/generate.

## Product Purpose

Cubik provides a polished dice-rolling, card-drawing, and Vimshottari dasha-generation tool with clear results, a visible total for dice, separate deck modes for cards, selectable Mahadasha for dasha generation, and a randomness implementation that avoids predictable patterns.
The app includes a lightweight personal access-code gate that remembers approved access on the same browser/device.

## Brand Personality

Dark, premium, precise. The interface should feel like a quiet black gaming table with bright physical dice, clean physical cards, and structured Jyotish period output, not a toy page.

## Anti-references

Avoid casino clutter, neon overload, childish board-game visuals, generic purple gradients, and explanation-heavy landing-page sections.

## Design Principles

- Keep the current random object, dice, cards, or dasha chain, as the primary object on screen.
- Make the roll/draw feel physical, but keep interaction fast.
- Use a restrained palette: black surface, white dice, one cool accent.
- Show enough result context without turning the page into a dashboard.
- Preserve real randomness behavior: repeated results can happen, predictable patterns should not.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Respect reduced-motion preferences by disabling roll animation and showing results instantly.
